Section 01 — Purpose

About This Project

My name is Micheal Ray Berry. This website documents my public weight loss and accountability project: 340 pounds to 175 pounds.

I did not need more information, another private plan, or another promise to myself. I needed a structure that made my actions visible, measurable, and difficult to quietly abandon. In private, I could delay, renegotiate, restart, or stop recording when the results became uncomfortable. This project exists to remove those escape routes.

I respond to external structure, immediate verification, fixed deadlines, and consequences that cannot be casually negotiated in the moment. The project takes the decision away from the tired, discouraged, impulsive version of me and places it in rules established by the clear-minded version of me.

Every day produces a public record — successful or not — so progress is based on documented action rather than intention.

Up, down, or flat — it gets posted.

Micheal Ray Berry. This record uses my real name and my real face.
Full disclosure

The Structure Behind This Record

This project is not self-directed. The Accountability Partner administers the official record, and I do not have unilateral authority to alter what that record says.

I built this project and this website, and then I handed control of the official record to someone else. That was the point. Previous attempts had the same weakness: I set the rules, which meant I could also move them. Under this project, the daily inspections, uniform, documentation standards, corrective requirements, and public record of failure operate under an agreement that I do not get to renegotiate on a bad day.

What I gave up is discretion over whether the results are documented and how compliance is judged.

The Accountability Partner administers the requirements, reviews the record, and determines whether a documented requirement has been satisfied. When a violation occurs, it remains part of the record. Every entry on this site is me presenting the required evidence for review by someone who is not me, because a record that I alone could revise whenever it became uncomfortable would defeat the purpose.

The uniform is part of that documentation standard: a plain black unitard, consistent shoes, and the same four recorded angles. The goal is to make records from different days directly comparable without changing the presentation to make a particular result look better or worse.

This is published under my real name because the accountability is intended to be real and attributable. I participate in the project; I do not unilaterally control its official record. That separation is the mechanism.

Voluntarily established and governed by the signed agreement below. The arrangement ends only by completion or its terms (§11, §13).

02

Why My Real Name Is Used

This project is attached to my real legal name because anonymity would weaken the accountability. In the past, it has been too easy to restart privately, hide failure, change direction, or abandon the process without a lasting public record.

The point is not branding. The point is consequence.

03

Why the Attire Is Standardized

Required photos and videos are recorded in the project uniform so that changes are visible, comparisons are consistent, and the record cannot be softened by flattering clothing, angles, or presentation.

The purpose is not fashion. The purpose is honesty.

04

Daily Documentation

Every day by 10:00 PM Eastern: a four-angle inspection video published to the official record and posted publicly to YouTube, four daily photos, a weight entry, and a tracker update. Daily documentation exists because long gaps create room for avoidance.

The required update is either completed or it is not.

05

Progress and Milestones

Daily weigh-ins in the public tracker and documented milestone weigh-ins at 300, 275, 250, 225, 200, and 175 pounds track whether the project is actually working. Weight fluctuation is recorded as progress information — only missing or false documentation is a violation.

The weight loss itself is conducted under a physician's care, at a sustainable rate. The project enforces documentation — the medicine is supervised separately.

The record is based on visible documentation and measurable results — not motivation, explanations, or intentions.

06

Missed Requirements and Consequences

Missed requirements are documented publicly. If I fail to meet a requirement, the miss is recorded permanently in the public Violation Log — the date, the requirement missed, the status, when the correction was submitted, when it was resolved, and the Accountability Partner's verification result. The correction is public too: corner time, recorded in one unbroken take, posted to the official channel, and published beside the entry that caused it — and it stays there after it is served. A consequence nobody can see is one I would eventually learn to discount, and I have discounted enough already.

The purpose is not drama. The purpose is enforcement.

07

The Public Record

This website is the official public record of the project. Posts, updates, photos, weigh-ins, missed requirements, and corrective sessions are part of the record. It exists to prevent quiet abandonment, hidden failure, or selective documentation.

The record begins here and continues until the goal is reached.

08

Why the Record Is Permanent

A record I could delete later is not a record — it is a draft. If an exit existed, every hard day would point toward it: quit, clean up, pretend it never happened. Permanence closes that door. Quitting no longer erases anything, so the only version of the future where these photos end well is the one where the later photos show 175.

That is why the record is deliberately built to operate without my cooperation. It is administered by the Accountability Partner, not by me. It is mirrored across independent archives — this website, the public tracker, the YouTube channel, and a public photo archive — each with its own copy. It is indexed by search engines under my real name, on purpose, so it can be found by anyone who looks. I cannot soften it, edit it, or take it down, and any attempt to would itself become part of the record.

Maximum exposure is not a side effect. It is the mechanism. The more findable this record is, the more it costs to abandon — and the more the only way out is through.

The archive cannot be edited. The next photo can.

Questions, answered plainly

Is this real?

Yes. Real name, real face, real signed agreement, daily since August 13, 2026. Every claim on this site is verifiable against the public tracker, the photo archive, and the video archive.

Is this voluntary?

Entirely. I designed this system, asked for it in writing, and signed it — precisely because voluntary private promises kept failing. The Accountability Partner administers the structure; he did not impose it.

What happens if he misses a requirement?

A Violation Event is entered permanently in the public log: the date, the requirement missed, the status — open, corrected, or resolved — the submission and resolution timestamps, and the Accountability Partner's verification result. Corner time follows, recorded and published beside the entry. Submitting it resolves the entry; the AP reviews the posting and can overrule, reopening it. The entry stays forever.

Can he delete this site or quietly quit?

No. The website, domain, trackers, and violation log are owned and administered by the Accountability Partner for the project's duration. Thirty days of silence or any attempt to take the record down converts the site to a permanent public abandonment record.

Is the weight loss medically supervised?

Yes — the weight loss proceeds under a physician's care at a sustainable rate. The project enforces documentation, not medicine. Violations are documentation failures; ordinary weight fluctuation is never a violation.

When does it end?

One way: 175 pounds, held for 28 consecutive days under the verification standard. Then the site converts to a permanent completion record. There is no other ending that isn't public.

I know him personally. What now?

Then you're part of how this works. Recognition is not a risk of this project — it's the mechanism. Questions and reports go to ap@michealrayberry.com, answered by the Accountability Partner, not by Micheal.
