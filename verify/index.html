The record can prove itself.

Every photo and video on this record is fingerprinted at the moment of capture: the tool computes the file's SHA-256 hash and logs it — with Google's server clock, not a device clock — to an append-only sheet only the Accountability Partner controls. If a file were ever edited, re-shot, or swapped afterward, its fingerprint would no longer match the one logged at capture time.

Server-anchored time

Capture events are timestamped by Google's servers. The device clock — the thing a backdater would change — is never trusted.

One-time challenge codes

Each session fetches a code that did not exist until seconds before recording — spoken aloud and burned into the video. Pre-recorded or replayed footage cannot contain it.

Content fingerprints

SHA-256 hashes of every file, logged at capture. The checker below recomputes a file's hash in your browser and searches the public log for it.
