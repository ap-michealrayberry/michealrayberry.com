Section 01 — Purpose

About This Project

My name is Micheal Ray Berry. This website documents my public weight loss and accountability project: 340 pounds to 175 pounds.

I did not need more information, another private plan, or another promise to myself. I needed a structure that made my actions visible, measurable, and difficult to quietly abandon. In private, I could delay, renegotiate, restart, or stop recording when the results became uncomfortable. This project exists to remove those escape routes.

I respond to external structure, immediate verification, fixed deadlines, and consequences that cannot be casually negotiated in the moment. The project takes the decision away from the tired, discouraged, impulsive version of me and places it in rules established by the clear-minded version of me.

Every day produces a public record — successful or not — so progress is based on documented action rather than intention.

Up, down, or flat — it gets posted.

Micheal Ray Berry. This record uses my real name and my real face.
02

Why My Real Name Is Used

This project is attached to my real legal name because anonymity would weaken the accountability. In the past, it has been too easy to restart privately, hide failure, change direction, or abandon the process without a lasting public record.

The point is not branding. The point is consequence.

03

Why the Attire Is Standardized

Required photos and videos are recorded in the project uniform so that changes are visible, comparisons are consistent, and the record cannot be softened by flattering clothing, angles, or presentation.

The purpose is not fashion. The purpose is honesty.

04

Daily Documentation

Every day by 10:00 PM Eastern: a four-angle inspection video on YouTube, four daily photos, a weight entry, and a tracker update. Daily documentation exists because long gaps create room for avoidance.

The required update is either completed or it is not.

05

Progress and Milestones

Daily weigh-ins in the public tracker and documented milestone weigh-ins at 300, 275, 250, 225, 200, and 175 pounds track whether the project is actually working. Weight fluctuation is recorded as progress information — only missing or false documentation is a violation.

The weight loss itself is conducted under a physician's care, at a sustainable rate. The project enforces documentation — the medicine is supervised separately.

The record is based on visible documentation and measurable results — not motivation, explanations, or intentions.

06

Missed Requirements and Consequences

Missed requirements are documented publicly. If I fail to meet a requirement, the miss is recorded permanently in the public Violation Log — the date, the nature of the failure, and whether it is resolved. The consequence itself is administered privately by the Accountability Partner under the signed Penalty Schedule; amounts, terms, and details are never published. Accountability without consequence is just commentary.

The purpose is not drama. The purpose is enforcement.

07

The Public Record

This website is the official public record of the project. Posts, updates, photos, weigh-ins, missed requirements, and penalties are part of the record. It exists to prevent quiet abandonment, hidden failure, or selective documentation.

The record begins here and continues until the goal is reached.

08

Why the Record Is Permanent

A record I could delete later is not a record — it is a draft. If an exit existed, every hard day would point toward it: quit, clean up, pretend it never happened. Permanence closes that door. Quitting no longer erases anything, so the only version of the future where these photos end well is the one where the later photos show 175.

That is why the record is deliberately built to operate without my cooperation. It is administered by the Accountability Partner, not by me. It is mirrored across independent archives — this website, the public tracker, YouTube, and a public photo archive — each with its own copy, none of which I control. It is indexed by search engines under my real name, on purpose, so it can be found by anyone who looks. I cannot soften it, edit it, or take it down, and any attempt to would itself become part of the record.

Maximum exposure is not a side effect. It is the mechanism. The more findable this record is, the more it costs to abandon — and the more the only way out is through.

The archive cannot be edited. The next photo can.

1. Purpose and Nature of the Agreement

This is a voluntary, non-commercial personal accountability agreement. Micheal Ray Berry has determined that private plans left room to delay, restart, and disappear when the record became uncomfortable, and enters this Agreement to remove that escape. The Agreement governs documentation and verification: it makes Micheal Ray Berry's actions visible, measurable, and difficult to quietly abandon. It does not prescribe diet, exercise, or rate of loss, which remain governed by §6.4 (medical care). This Agreement supersedes all earlier drafts, versions, and public announcements.

2. Definitions

Project Day — each calendar day in the America/New_York time zone, beginning Day 1. Official Platforms — the website michealrayberry.com, the YouTube channel @michealrayberry, and the X account @michealrayberry; only records on Official Platforms count toward compliance. Public Trackers — the AP-administered Google Drive records comprising the weigh-in log, Violation Log, photo archive, and Updates log, together with the website pages automatically generated or updated from those records. Daily Compliance Packet — the four items listed in §3, due every Project Day. Violation Event — a documentation failure as defined in §7; weight values are never Violation Events.

3. The Daily Compliance Packet

Every Project Day, by 10:00 PM Eastern Time, Micheal Ray Berry shall complete all four items: (1) Daily Inspection Video — one standardized inspection video published to the official YouTube channel, recorded with the official Recording Assistant at michealrayberry.com/#record, and only if it is verifiably unavailable an AP-approved equivalent (§4.4); (2) Daily Accountability Photos — the standardized four-angle photos (front, left side, rear, right side) from the same session, uploaded to the designated Google Drive folder by 10:00 PM Eastern Time; (3) Weight Entry — that day's weight entered into the designated Google Drive weigh-in tracker by 10:00 PM Eastern Time; (4) Tracker Update — all other fields assigned to Micheal Ray Berry in the designated Google Drive tracker completed and saved by 10:00 PM Eastern Time. Multiple missed items on the same Project Day constitute one Violation Event. Timely entry or upload to the designated Google Drive location constitutes completion of the applicable requirement; Google Drive timestamps and revision history are authoritative evidence of timely submission. A delay or failure in the website's automated publication process does not constitute a Violation Event if the underlying Drive record was complete and timely — in that case the AP shall restore publication without penalty to Micheal Ray Berry.

4. Documentation Standard

4.1 Uniform. All official documentation is recorded in the project uniform: solid black unitard and plain black shoes. Identity is established by Micheal Ray Berry's visible face and the Official Platforms. 4.2 Pose and angles. Standing upright, hands behind head, body and face visible. Each Daily Inspection documents front, left side, rear, and right side views, closing on a front view. A normal Daily Inspection runs approximately 20–60 seconds; no specific length is required if identity, attire, pose, angles, and visibility are clear. 4.3 No concealment. No cropping, filters, props, lighting, editing, clothing alteration, camera angle, timing, or platform setting intended to conceal body size, identity, attire, weight status, or compliance. The same standards apply to the daily photos. 4.4 Capture instrument. All Daily Inspection documentation — the inspection video and the four daily photos — shall be captured using the official Recording Assistant at michealrayberry.com/#record, which enforces the standardized sequence, pose, angles, and on-record overlay. An AP-approved equivalent may be used only when the Recording Assistant is verifiably unavailable; such an outage is handled under §9 and is not a Violation Event.

5.1 An Official Weigh-In is conducted only to verify a milestone (§6.1) or the completion period (§6.3): on camera, on the same scale, under the full §4 standard. Daily weight entries under §3 are self-reported and serve as progress information. 5.2 A monthly progress review is published to the Updates log by the AP.

6. Milestones, Completion, and Health

6.1 Milestones: 300 · 275 · 250 · 225 · 200 · 175 lb. A milestone counts only when documented by an Official Weigh-In and a milestone video. 6.2 Reset. Each documented milestone resets the accumulated Violation Event count to zero for future violations. 7. Violation Events

A Violation Event is exclusively a documentation failure: a required record that is missed, late, incomplete, false, manipulated, concealed, deleted, or refused, including failure to complete an assigned consequence under §8. Ordinary weight fluctuation, plateaus, or temporary gains are progress information and are never Violation Events. The AP determines whether a Violation Event occurred by applying the written requirements of this Agreement to available timestamps, recordings, tracker records, and other objective evidence, and shall provide Micheal Ray Berry a written notice identifying the failed requirement and supporting evidence. Micheal Ray Berry may submit evidence of compliance or a documented exception within 48 hours; after reviewing it, the AP issues a final written determination. Disagreement, embarrassment, regret, or dissatisfaction with a requirement is not grounds to reverse a properly documented determination.

8. Consequences — Private Administration

8.1 Each qualifying Violation Event triggers a consequence under the separate signed Penalty Schedule, administered and verified privately by the Accountability Partner. A completed consequence does not erase the underlying Violation Event. A weekly weight gain may trigger a consequence only after review by the AP. 8.2 Confidentiality. The nature, amount, terms, deadlines, receipts, footage, and all communications concerning any consequence remain permanently confidential between the parties and shall not be published on any platform. 8.3 The Violation Log — the AP-administered public record — states for each Violation Event only: its date, the nature of the documentation failure, and whether it is resolved or unresolved. Public log entries are permanent; corrections are appended, never substituted.

9. Documented Exceptions

A requirement may be excused or postponed only because of a documented medical event, a genuine emergency, a verified failure of an Official Platform, or a verified failure of required Project equipment or systems (the designated scale, the Recording Assistant, camera, or archive) outside Micheal Ray Berry's reasonable control, reported to the AP as soon as reasonably possible and ordinarily within 24 hours, supported by reasonable proof. When the interruption ends, the AP designates one outcome: (a) the requirement is excused, where later completion would be impossible, medically inappropriate, or misleading; (b) the requirement is completed within 24 hours as a clearly labeled late replacement record; or (c) an equivalent record is completed within 24 hours. Travel, ordinary work demands, scheduling conflicts, embarrassment, lack of preparation, or avoidable technical problems are not documented exceptions. The public log identifies an accepted exception without disclosing private medical details.
