About · why this is public

Why I chose public accountability

Micheal Ray Berry asked for this structure in writing. {{ agreementStatusSentence }} The Accountability Partner holds the site, the domain, the record and the accounts; Micheal files to it and cannot edit it.

The published daily standard is a weight entry, four photographs, an inspection video, and a tracker update by 10:00 PM ET. The supervision schedule is 6:00–10:00 PM ET, Sunday–Thursday. Public testing exercises these requirements separately from the official record. Possible misses are flagged automatically. The Accountability Partner verifies each Violation Event before assigning a recorded correction.

In his words

“I am deliberately making this accountability public. I want this project seen, found, shared, discussed, and followed under my real name. Visibility creates pressure I cannot quietly escape. I consent to people observing this public record and holding me to the commitments I have voluntarily made.”

“I did not need more information, another private plan, or another promise to myself. I needed a structure that made my actions visible, measurable, and difficult to quietly abandon. In private, I could delay, renegotiate, restart, or stop recording when the results became uncomfortable. This project exists to remove those escape routes.”

“Someone recognizing me from this project is not a security failure or an accidental side effect. Within the boundaries of the information I have deliberately chosen to publish, recognition is part of the accountability mechanism.”

“I chose to make the commitment public because I wanted a promise that would be harder to break than one made only to myself.”

Questions, answered plainly

Is this real?

The real-name public record began {{ startDateLong }}. Published entries link to their supporting tracker, photograph, and video records; the separate agreement page reports current execution status.

I know him personally. What now?

Then yes — this really is the Micheal Ray Berry you know, and the results are under his real name.

Recognition is an intended part of the public project. In ordinary personal settings, people who know him may ask about the published record; workplace contact is excluded: ask whether today’s documentation is filed, whether he served the corner time he owes, or whether he attended Evening Supervision. He published the rules so that people who know him can know what he owes. See Hold Me Accountable.

If you notice an inconsistency or a possible breach, report it through the Observer Submission page (no name or email required) or ap@michealrayberry.com. The Accountability Partner reviews it; Micheal does not.

Holding him to the record is welcome. Harassment, threats, contact with his employer or coworkers, attempts to obtain private information, or showing up uninvited is not.

Is this voluntary?

Entirely. I designed this system and requested it in writing because voluntary private promises kept failing. {{ agreementStatusSentence }}

Can he delete this site or quietly quit?

The Accountability Partner administers the current site, domain, and record. Published versions can be archived and timestamped, but privacy, safety, consent-withdrawal, and lawful redaction or takedown remain available. Edition 2 describes how a sustained end to participation is documented when its terms apply.

Is the weight loss medically supervised?

Health decisions are handled separately from this site. This public record documents accountability requirements; it does not provide medical advice or independently verify medical supervision.

When does it end?

Edition 2 defines completion as {{ goalWeight }} pounds held for {{ completionDays }} consecutive days and confirmed through its review process while active. Other endings may be documented, subject to current consent, privacy, and safety rules.

What does the record show?

The record may show dated weigh-ins and published photographs or video. Edition 2 also describes an issue log and corrective recordings when active. Verification photographs stay private.
