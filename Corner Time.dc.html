Corner Time

Project uniform — solid black unitard, plain black shoes (the pink Penalty Uniform instead if the AP has assigned it under Amendment No. 5). Standing upright, facing the designated corner, full body and corner continuously in frame. Structure per §8.2: opening acknowledgment, silent corrective period, closing acknowledgment. One continuous take — pausing, leaving frame, breaking posture, refusing the acknowledgments, or ending early makes the session incomplete.

Violation — from the public log
The public Violation Log is clean. Corner time is only performed against a logged violation with an AP-approved assignment.
Assigned level — as approved by the AP
Set this to the level the AP approved for the selected violation. The duration is fixed by level and cannot be shortened or extended.
Position check — before you begin
Phone propped, portrait orientation · full body visible head to shoes · the corner itself in frame · uniform on · nothing else in the frame. At the tone, read the opening acknowledgment from the screen, then face the corner for the silent period; at the closing tone, turn and read the closing acknowledgment. You may stop for safety at any time — a safety stop is never a violation; it is reviewed by the AP.
