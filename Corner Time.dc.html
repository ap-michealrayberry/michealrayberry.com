Corrective requirement · recorded · privately verified · max one session per calendar day

Corner Time

Project uniform — solid black unitard, plain black shoes; the camera checks for it. Standing upright, facing the designated corner, full body and corner continuously in frame. Structure: opening statement, silent corrective period, closing statement — fully AI-voiced, and you do not speak at any point (the guiding voice, AI or device, is captured on the recording). One continuous take — pausing, leaving frame, breaking posture, or ending early makes the session incomplete.

Violation — from the public log
The public Violation Log is clean. Corner time is only performed against a logged violation with an AP-approved assignment.
Assigned level — as approved by the AP
Set this to the level the AP approved for the selected violation. The duration is fixed by level and cannot be shortened or extended.
Position check — before you begin
Phone propped, portrait orientation · full body visible head to shoes · the corner itself in frame · uniform on · nothing else in the frame. At the tone, stand at attention while the voice states the assignment, then face the corner for the silent period; at the closing tone, turn back and stand at attention while the voice closes the session. You never speak. During the silent period the camera monitors position, posture (hands behind the head), stillness, and uniform — breaches draw spoken warnings; three warnings invalidate the session and nothing is saved. You may stop for safety at any time — a safety stop is never a violation; it is reviewed by the AP.
