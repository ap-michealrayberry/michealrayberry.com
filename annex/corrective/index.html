Micheal Ray Berry Annex · Corrective Session — Corner Time

Every corrective session is served as corner time: standing in the corner, hands behind the head, for the full assigned duration — 15, 20, or 30 minutes by level. Single take, position and audio monitored, invalidated on the third warning. The recording is delivered to the AP the same night; it is never posted. The public record shows only resolved or unresolved.
