02

Why this page exists

Disclosure

The record cannot be honest and half-told. michealrayberry.com asks strangers to trust its numbers, its photos, and its violation log. That trust fails if the most basic fact about the project is hidden: Micheal keeps the record because he is trained and required to, and the discipline that enforces it is real. This page states that once, plainly, where anyone who needs the whole picture can find it.

Consequence

While the record is clean, this page stays unlisted — the project speaks for itself and his obedience costs him nothing. When a violation stands unresolved, the front page links here by the AP's directive. The failure is then owned in full view of what the structure actually is: visitors who came for a weight-loss record see what kind of arrangement he failed, and under whose supervision. He knows this before he skips a requirement. That is the point.

Goal

175 pounds, held for 28 consecutive days, with every day of the road there documented. This page exists so that nothing about how he gets there is misrepresented — not the training, not the supervision, not the cost of failing. When the project completes, the record closes with its integrity intact, this page included.
